Format: A solo, branching adventure gamebook for one player character, using numbered entries, meaningful choices, skill checks, combat, and persistent consequences.

A lone adventurer arrives in the isolated settlement of Greyfen after reports of a bell ringing from deep within the surrounding wetlands. No bell tower is known to exist there, yet each night the sound grows louder and people begin disappearing.
The adventurer must investigate Greyfen, cross the dangerous swamp, uncover ruins hidden beneath the marsh, and discover what is causing the bell to ring.
The journey gradually reveals that the bell is connected to an ancient structure buried below the wetlands, and that someone in Greyfen may know far more about it than they admit.
The adventure ends with the adventurer confronting the source of the ringing and deciding what should happen to the power hidden beneath Greyfen.

This is for a level 1 character
