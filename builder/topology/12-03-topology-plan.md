TOPOLOGY: 12-03
TYPE: Nested two-stage choice with four distinct route outcomes

PRIMARY CHOICE:
Entry 01 presents exactly two materially different approaches.

PRIMARY ROUTES:
A: Entry 02
B: Entry 03

SECONDARY CHOICES:
Entry 02 must present two meaningful Route-A-specific options.
Entry 03 must present two meaningful Route-B-specific options.

SECONDARY ROUTES:
A1: 04 -> 08
A2: 05 -> 09
B1: 06 -> 10
B2: 07 -> 11

CROSS-BRANCH LINKS:
None before Entry 12.

CHOICE QUALITY:
04 versus 05 and 06 versus 07 must represent genuinely
different actions, approaches, or decisions.

They must not be cosmetic wording variants.

CONSEQUENCE ENTRIES:
08, 09, 10 and 11 must provide meaningful payoff for the
specific route taken.

Each should differ in at least one meaningful respect such as:
- information
- risk
- resource cost
- opportunity
- position
- NPC response
- discovery
- obstacle
- immediate gameplay experience

MECHANICAL INTERACTIONS:
Checks, saves, combat or other mechanics may occur where
appropriate, but are not required purely to justify an entry.

Do not insert a roll whose outcomes are functionally identical.

PRIMARY BRANCH LIFESPAN:
The distinction created at Entry 01 must remain meaningful
until Entry 12.

SECONDARY BRANCH LIFESPAN:
The distinction created at Entries 02/03 must remain meaningful
through its corresponding consequence entry.

GLOBAL RECONVERGENCE:
08, 09, 10 and 11 -> 12.

ENTRY 12:
Must establish a genuinely shared situation and introduce a
new development, arrival, threat, discovery, decision point,
or other meaningful beat.

It must not exist solely as a routing node.

LINEAR RUN:
No more than two consecutive purely linear passages.

PERSISTENT STATE:
Normally none.

All distinctions that do not legitimately persist beyond the
sequence should resolve before Entry 12.

SEQUENCE HANDOFF:
Entry 12 may rely only on facts common to every continuing route.
