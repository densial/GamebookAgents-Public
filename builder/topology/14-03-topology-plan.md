## Play-experience contract

- **This topology is a two-phase or double-diamond structure.** It deliberately reaches a genuine shared midpoint at Node 07, then gives the player a new independent decision or challenge based on the common state established there.
- **Node 01 is the first meaningful split.** Its two outcomes must represent genuinely different approaches, risks, methods, or opportunity profiles. Tone, wording, order of inquiry, or descriptive flavour alone do not qualify.
- **Nodes 02 and 03 are first-phase branch-local resolution points.** Each must produce two mechanically or materially meaningful outcomes. A split may be a player choice or a success/failure resolution, but it must affect survival, material outcome, an approved persistent-state opportunity/use, or the mechanically distinct challenge/consequence that follows.
- **Nodes 04, 05, and 06 are first-phase consequence/payoff entries.** Node 05 is intentionally shared by one outcome from each first-phase approach. It may be used only when those incoming outcomes have reached a genuinely compatible story and gameplay state.
- **Node 07 is a true global midpoint reconvergence.** It must be literally valid from Nodes 04, 05, and 06. It must not assume which first-phase route was taken or which first-phase result occurred.
- **Node 07 must also establish a genuine new common development before the second split.** The second half must not simply repeat the first decision in different words.
- Ordinary character-sheet consequences from the first phase may persist through Node 07, such as HP, conditions, expended resources, or approved material changes. Route-specific knowledge, local staging, unapproved tags, and incompatible possessions may not be silently universalised.
- **Node 07 then creates the second meaningful split to Nodes 08 and 09.** This second choice or challenge must be based only on information and circumstances common to every route reaching Node 07.
- **Nodes 08 and 09 are second-phase branch-local resolution points.** Their outcomes must again create meaningful gameplay differences rather than cosmetic branches.
- **Nodes 10, 11, and 12 are second-phase consequence/payoff entries.** Node 11 is intentionally shared by one outcome from each second-phase approach and therefore requires compatible incoming state.
- **Node 13 is the final global reconvergence.** It must make literal sense from Nodes 10, 11, and 12 while preserving any ordinary character-state differences that should remain.
- **Node 14 is shared development/handoff.** It must move the adventure forward, prepare the next sequence, deliver an approved common reveal, or otherwise provide useful progression. It must not merely restate Node 13 or announce the end of the sequence.
- Every complete continuing route contains eight entries.
- The first and second halves should feel like distinct stages of the same sequence: first resolve one problem, gain common progression at Node 07, then face a new decision or challenge created by that progression.
- Do not use Node 07 as a fake reconvergence that immediately depends on route-specific facts. If the second phase requires knowing which first-phase branch occurred, the planned content is incompatible with this topology unless that distinction is carried by ordinary authorised state and explicitly supported.
- Core story information required to continue must not be gated behind success. Success and failure should primarily change survival risk, material outcome, or another authorised gameplay consequence while progression remains available.
- Do not add cross-links that are not listed above. The fixed graph and this play-experience contract are both authoritative.
