## Play-experience contract

- **Node 01 is a shared opening/setup entry.** It must establish the immediate situation and lead naturally to the first meaningful decision at Node 02. It must not exist merely as padding.
- **Node 02 is the primary split.** Its two outcomes must create genuinely different playable approaches, methods, risks, or opportunity profiles. Tone, wording, order of inquiry, or descriptive framing alone are not sufficient.
- **Nodes 03 and 04 develop the selected primary approach.** Each must make its route materially distinct before the branch-local resolution point. They must not be interchangeable pass-throughs.
- **Nodes 05 and 06 are branch-local resolution points.** Each has two outcomes, and those outcomes must differ meaningfully in survival risk, material outcome, approved persistent-state opportunity/use, or the mechanically distinct challenge/consequence that follows.
- **Nodes 07/08 and 09/10 are route-specific consequence or payoff entries.** They must make the result of Nodes 05/06 perceptible before any cross-route reconvergence.
- **This topology deliberately braids equivalent consequence states across the two primary approaches.** Node 11 receives from Nodes 07 and 10; Node 12 receives from Nodes 08 and 09. These cross-route reconvergences are valid only when the incoming routes have reached genuinely compatible story and gameplay states.
- **Node 11 and Node 12 must remain meaningfully different from one another.** They should represent two distinct consequence states, opportunity states, positions, or levels of survival/material advantage. They must not be duplicate prose leading to the same result.
- **Node 13 is the first global reconvergence.** It must be literally valid from both Node 11 and Node 12, preserve any ordinary character-state differences that should remain, and establish genuine common progression.
- **Node 14 is a shared development/handoff entry after global reconvergence.** It must add useful progression, positioning, revelation, preparation, or transition into the next sequence. It must not merely restate Node 13 or announce the end of the sequence.
- Every complete continuing route contains eight entries. Do not create shortcuts, extra links, or hidden bypasses that undermine the intended branch lifespan.
- A split may represent a player choice or a mechanical success/failure resolution, but every split must have a clear gameplay reason. Do not use this topology for cosmetic branching.
- Core story progress must remain available on every viable continuing route. Better and worse outcomes should primarily differ through survival and/or material value rather than by gating information required to continue.
- Cross-route reconvergence must never universalise route-specific knowledge, possessions, NPC relationships, or local state. If two incoming routes cannot truthfully share Node 11 or Node 12, the planned content is incompatible with this topology and must be revised upstream rather than papered over.
- Do not add cross-links that are not listed above. The fixed graph is the topology contract.
