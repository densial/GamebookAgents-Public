## Play-experience contract

- **Node 01 is the primary split.** Its two outcomes must create genuinely different playable approaches or consequence paths, not merely different tone, wording, order of inquiry, or descriptive framing.
- **The two primary routes remain separate until Node 14.** Nodes on one primary route must not assume events, facts, possessions, or local state from the other route.
- **Nodes 04 and 05 are branch-local resolution points.** Each has two outcomes that must differ meaningfully in survival risk, material outcome, approved persistent-state opportunity/use, or the mechanically distinct challenge that follows.
- **Nodes 06/07 reconverge at Node 10; Nodes 08/09 reconverge at Node 11.** Nodes 10 and 11 must acknowledge the common result of their incoming outcomes without erasing any ordinary character-state consequence that should persist.
- **Nodes 12 and 13 are branch-specific development or payoff entries after local reconvergence.** They must add useful progression, consequence, discovery, positioning, or preparation. They must not exist merely to consume an entry.
- **Node 14 is the first global reconvergence.** It must contain a genuine common development or handoff that is valid from both primary routes and must not rely on route-exclusive knowledge or possessions.
- A split may represent a player choice or a mechanical success/failure resolution, but every split must have a clear gameplay reason. Do not use this topology to support cosmetic branching.
- Core story progress must remain available on every viable continuing route. Better and worse outcomes should primarily differ through survival and/or material value rather than by gating information required to continue.
- Do not add cross-links that are not listed above. The fixed graph is the topology contract.
