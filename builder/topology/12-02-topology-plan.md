CROSS-BRANCH LINKS:
None before Entry 12.

OUTCOMES:
03 -> 06 / 07
04 -> 08 / 09
05 -> 10 / 11

Each pair must represent a meaningful difference in at least one of:
- information
- risk
- resource expenditure
- opportunity
- position
- obstacle

- route experience
- other gameplay-relevant consequence

FAIL-FORWARD:
Preferred where appropriate.

Failure/alternate outcomes must not simply reproduce
the successful outcome with less flattering wording.

GLOBAL RECONVERGENCE:
06–11 -> 12

ENTRY 12:
First globally shared entry after the primary choice.

All branch-specific consequences that cannot legitimately
persist beyond the sequence must be resolved before or within
their respective outcome entry.

Entry 12 must introduce a genuine common development,
arrival, discovery, threat, decision point, or other new beat.

It must not exist solely to merge graph edges.

LINEAR RUN:
Maximum one purely linear entry after the primary choice
before another meaningful interaction/outcome.

PERSISTENT STATE:
Normally none.

SEQUENCE HANDOFF:
Entry 12 contains only facts valid for every continuing route.
